Provider rates Malaysian, Singapore and Thai banks, platforms and dealer quotes

Each provider is priced as live mid minus a margin (the methodology's markup model), unless you type a quote for a specific pair. A typed quote is checked against a sanity band of −25 to +500 bps from mid, which catches per-100 and inverted-pair mistakes.

Transfer fees and SWIFT Fees are in the sending currency

Defaults follow the sheet example: SGD 20 sender fee, SGD 10 intermediary fee, 1 day transit. The USD correspondent fee and the extra days are illustrative.

Default SWIFT charge code

OUR: you pay everything

Sender and intermediary fees are added on top. The beneficiary gets the full amount. Best for supplier invoices.

SHA: shared

You pay your bank's fee on top. Intermediary and landing fees come out of the amount on the way.

BEN: beneficiary pays

All fees come out of the amount. You pay nothing extra; they receive the least.

Methodology How the dashboard works it out

Rate types

A mid (central-bank reference or aggregator) is the fair market rate. A board rate, platform rate or dealer quote is what a customer actually gets. Only mids feed the consensus; customer rates never do.

Consensus mid
first = median(fresh mids)
used  = sources within 50 bps of first
mid   = median(used)
spread = largest |rate − mid| of a used source, in bps

Fresh means at most 2 business days old. If nothing is fresh, older rates are used and labelled stale; if there are none, the fallback rates are used.

Provider margin (markup model)
margin_bps = (mid − customer_rate) / mid × 10,000
customer_rate = live_mid × (1 − margin_bps / 10,000)

Margins change less than rates, so storing a margin and re-applying it to the live mid keeps estimates current.

Routes

A route is a chain of legs: transfers (TT) and conversions. A: convert in the sending country, then send. B: send the source currency to your own account abroad, then convert there. C: convert a balance already held abroad. Via USD: two conversions with a US correspondent. Platform: local pay-in and payout. Dealer RFQ: negotiated rate at the sending bank.

Fees and SWIFT codes
OUR: sender + intermediary on top; landing fee deducted
SHA: sender on top; intermediary + landing deducted
BEN: all deducted

On-top fees raise what you pay; deducted fees shrink what gets converted or received.

Comparing routes fairly
effective_rate = net_received / total_paid
net_per_amount = effective_rate × amount
cost_bps = (total_paid × mid − net_received) / (total_paid × mid) × 10,000
edge = net_per_amount(route) − net_per_amount(other)

Routes can cost slightly different totals, so they are scaled to the same amount before comparing.

Transit risk
daily_vol = annual_vol / √252
band = net_received × daily_vol × √(days unconverted)
P(edge lost) = Φ(−edge / band)

Assumes normal daily moves and constant volatility. Real FX has fat tails and event risk.

What this tool does not do

It compares and explains; it never moves money, does not guarantee a rate, and never suggests splitting a payment to stay under a threshold. Calculations use floating-point numbers rounded for display; the self-check above confirms they match the worked examples.
