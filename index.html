How to read the grade

GradeMargin vs midWhat it usually means
Sharpunder 15 bpsDealer or platform pricing. Hard to beat.
Fair15 to 50 bpsTypical negotiated corporate rate.
High50 to 150 bpsBoard rate. Ask for a dealer quote (RFQ).
Very highover 150 bpsRetail counter pricing, or a typing mistake.

Bands are this tool's rule of thumb, not an industry standard. Adjust your expectations for exotic pairs and small tickets.

What "Live" means here

Live means the sources answered on the last check. Most free sources publish daily, so numbers often stay the same between checks; Coinbase is the only intraday reference. Each source is called no more often than it updates, to respect the providers' terms. For dealing-grade intraday rates, add a paid feed or a bank API behind a small server.

How it works

The method, the words, and the shortcuts.

Rate types

A mid (central-bank reference or aggregator) is the fair market rate. A board rate, platform rate or dealer quote is what a customer actually gets. Only mids feed the consensus.

Consensus mid
first  = median(fresh mids)
used   = sources within 50 bps of first
mid    = median(used)
spread = largest |rate − mid| of a used source
Provider margin
margin_bps    = (mid − customer_rate) / mid × 10,000
customer_rate = live_mid × (1 − margin_bps / 10,000)

Margins move less than rates, so a stored margin is re-applied to today's mid.

Routes

A: convert in the sending country, then send. B: send the source currency to your own account abroad, convert there. C: convert a balance already held abroad. D: via USD (two conversions). P: FX platform with local payout. R: dealer quote at your sending bank.

Fees and SWIFT codes
OUR: sender + intermediary on top; landing deducted
SHA: sender on top; intermediary + landing deducted
BEN: all deducted
Comparing fairly
effective_rate = net_received / total_paid
cost_bps = (total_paid × mid − net_received) / (total_paid × mid) × 10,000
Transit risk
daily_vol = annual_vol / √252
band = net × daily_vol × √(days unconverted)
P(edge lost) = Φ(−edge / band)

Assumes normal daily moves. Real FX has fat tails and event risk.

What this tool does not do

It compares and explains. It never moves money, does not guarantee a rate, and never suggests splitting a payment to stay under a threshold.

Glossary

bps
Basis point = 0.01%. 50 bps on 1,000,000 = 5,000.
Mid
Halfway between buy and sell in the wholesale market.
TT
Telegraphic transfer: a SWIFT payment.
RFQ
Request for quote from a bank's dealing desk.
BIC
SWIFT code identifying a bank (8 or 11 characters).
Value date
The day the money actually settles.
