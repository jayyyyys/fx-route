What "Live" means

Live means every source was checked in the last minute. The free sources publish daily (Frankfurter, fawazahmed0) or at set sessions (BNM), so numbers often stay the same between checks. For minute-level rates, add a paid intraday feed or a bank API on the server.

Illustrative only. Not financial, legal or tax advice. Rates are indicative mid-market rates and estimated customer rates, not dealing rates. The tool compares; it never moves money.

Sources: Frankfurter (central-bank reference rates); Bank Negara Malaysia (data.gov.my, CC BY 4.0), served by Frankfurter; fawazahmed0/exchange-api (open data). Times are in your local time zone.
